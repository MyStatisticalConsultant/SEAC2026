# Curvigram — reporting template

Use this as a checklist when adapting the workshop method to your own data.

## Methods sentence

> The distribution of **[VARIABLE]** was examined using an area-normalised kernel curvigram. The baseline specification used a **[KERNEL]** kernel with observation-specific bandwidths defined as **[MULTIPLIER] × the reported ± measurement uncertainty**, evaluated over **[RANGE]** at **[STEP]** increments.

## Sensitivity sentence

> Robustness was assessed by repeating the analysis using **[LIST REASONABLE ALTERNATIVE KERNEL/BANDWIDTH SPECIFICATIONS]**.

## Results sentence

> The dominant concentration occurred near **[VALUE]°** and remained in approximately the same position across the sensitivity analyses, whereas **[SECONDARY FEATURE, IF PRESENT]** was more sensitive to smoothing choices.

## Interpretation sentence

> The dominant concentration was therefore treated as descriptively robust to the tested analytical specifications; however, the curvigram alone does not establish intentional astronomical targeting.

## Always report

- coordinate/variable;
- whether it is being treated as linear or circular;
- kernel;
- bandwidth rule;
- meaning of the uncertainty variable;
- bandwidth multiplier;
- vertical scaling;
- calculation range;
- calculation step;
- sensitivity specifications.

## Avoid

Do not write:

> “The peak proves that the monuments were intentionally aligned to [target].”

Prefer:

> “The analysis identifies a robust concentration near [value]. Any interpretation in terms of a specific astronomical target requires independent archaeological and contextual support.”
